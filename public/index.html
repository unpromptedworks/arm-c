1Statement

Every official number is a range with a name on it.

A jar says 454 g. A regulator says what “454 g” is allowed to mean. Someone argued over where that edge goes, and the edge is where the rule shows what it really protects. This site takes one tolerance at a time and works it through.

2Position taken

A tolerance is a decision about whose mistakes to forgive. Read one closely and you can tell who wrote it.

3How to read this form

Each entry gives the nominal value, what the rule allows, and the document that allows it. Entries marked worked have a page where you can set the variables and run the inspection yourself.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves it.

5Side by side

On the line (Form UE-2) puts the three worked entries through the same test: a lot that sits exactly on its rule’s line. A jar line passes 39 times in 40; a coin press 1 time in 2; a chocolate lot between 1 in 2 and about 1 in 5, depending on how patchy it is.
